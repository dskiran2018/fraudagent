What this system does

An agentic AI control for financial crime compliance (FCC) in an EU/BaFin-regulated institution. Every inbound cross-border payment is screened against consolidated sanctions and law-enforcement data, evaluated against the AML/CFT rulebook, and assigned a composite risk rating under a risk-based approach. Where the escalation threshold is breached, the case is auto-triaged and routed to the MLRO and executive compliance with a decision-ready case file — straight-through, with no Level 1 analyst in the loop.

  1. 1
    Payment Ingestion

    Normalises the inbound payment instruction from the core banking platform — originator, beneficiary, value, currency, corridor and payment rail — into a screening-ready record.

  2. 2
    Sanctions & Watchlist Screening

    Fuzzy name matching (rapidfuzz token-sort ratio) of originator and beneficiary against consolidated sanctions and law-enforcement data — OFAC SDN, EU Consolidated, UN Security Council, Interpol Red Notice, FBI Most Wanted and BaFin. Similarity ≥75% raises a hit; ≥95% is treated as a true match — thresholds tuned to balance detection coverage against false-positive volume.

  3. 3
    AML Rule Evaluation

    Tests the payment against the EU/German AML/CFT rulebook: threshold reporting above €10,000 (§11 GwG), originator-information completeness under the EU Funds Transfer Regulation 2015/847 (≥€1,000), FATF high-risk jurisdiction exposure, structuring (smurfing) indicators just below the reporting threshold, and 24-hour velocity anomalies.

  4. 4
    Composite Risk Rating

    Aggregates screening outcome, rule-breach severity, exposure value, jurisdiction risk and velocity into a single 0–100 inherent risk rating, banded MINIMAL to CRITICAL, which drives the downstream escalation decision.

  5. 5
    Alert Escalation

    Where the rating breaches 70 or a screening hit is confirmed, a priority-ranked alert is routed to the MLRO, Chief Compliance Officer and executive management with a recommended disposition — enhanced monitoring, payment hold with EDD, or freeze and immediate SAR filing.

  6. 6
    Case Disposition

    Produces an audit-ready case file: money-laundering typology classification, investigator narrative, the triggered reporting obligations (SAR/STR to the BaFin FIU via goAML), and a confidence score.
